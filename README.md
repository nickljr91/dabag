# DaBag

A bag window for **WoW: Forever** (client 1.60.1, interface 16001) where each bag can be reserved for one kind of item.

## Install

1. Copy the `DaBag` folder into your Forever client's `Interface\AddOns` folder (the same place Bagnon is installed).
2. Disable Bagnon on the character select screen (AddOns button). Two bag addons will fight over the B key.
3. Log in and press **B**.

## Launch and go

Bags are pre-assigned the first time a character logs in. Press **B**, then **Sort**.

| Bag | Holds |
|---|---|
| Backpack | Anything: quest items, odds and ends, overflow |
| Bag 1 | Gear |
| Bag 2 | Potions & Food |
| Bag 3 | Trade Goods |
| Bag 4 | Junk |

To change one, click **Bags**, click the bag's name and pick something else. Options > "Reset bags to the default layout" puts it back.

## Views

| Button | Shows |
|---|---|
| All | Every slot in one grid |
| Bags | One section per bag, with its assignment |
| Categories | Items grouped by category, wherever they are stored |
| Show bank | The saved copy of your bank, in any of the three views |

## Good to know

- **Automatic sorting** is on: new loot is filed into its bag about half a second after you pick it up. It waits while you are in combat, holding an item, or at a merchant, mailbox, trade, auction house or bank. Turn it off under Options.
- **Bank:** open your bank once; after that it is viewable anywhere. It is view-only. Use the normal bank window to move bank items.
- **Wrong category?** Alt+left-click an item and choose where it belongs. You can create your own categories there too.
- **Don't sort this bag** leaves a bag completely alone.
- Quivers, profession bags, the reagent bag and the keyring are shown but never sorted.
- Options: columns, window size, delete categories, view another character's bank.
- Commands: `/dab`, `/dab sort`, `/dab bank`, `/dab reset`.
